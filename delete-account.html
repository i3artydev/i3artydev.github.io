Delete your Memori Note account

Two ways to do it — in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Memori Note on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or by signing in again if you use Google or Apple.

Deletion happens immediately. There is no waiting period and no recovery — so export anything you want to keep first (Settings → Export notes).

Option 2 — By email

If you've already removed the app, or you can't sign in, email us from the address your account uses so we can confirm it's you:

i3arty.dev@gmail.com

Put “Account deletion request” in the subject. We'll confirm once it's done, normally within 7 days and always within 30.

What gets deleted

Permanently deleted

  • Your Memori Note account and its sign-in details
  • Your email address
  • Every note stored in the cloud for your account
  • The link between your account and your subscription

Not deleted — these stay with you

  • Notes on your own device. They remain in the app and are still yours. To remove them, delete the app or clear its data in your device settings.
  • Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel billing. Cancel separately in Google Play or the App Store, or you'll keep being charged.
  • Records we're required to keep by law, such as purchase records held by the app stores.

Cancel your subscription too

This is the step people miss. Account deletion and billing are separate systems — we can't cancel a subscription on your behalf.

  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Memori Note → Cancel
  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Memori Note → Cancel subscription

Before you go — save your notes

Deletion can't be undone. If you want a copy, export before you delete: Settings → Export notes, which gives you a JSON or CSV file you can keep or import elsewhere.
