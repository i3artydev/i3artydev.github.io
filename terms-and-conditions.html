3. Sync is not a backup service

Please read this one. Note sync keeps your notes consistent across your devices. It is a synchronisation feature, not a backup service.

Because it synchronises, a change or deletion made on one device is applied to your other devices and to the cloud copy. Deleting notes on one device deletes them everywhere. Sync cannot restore something you deleted.

We strongly recommend using the app's export feature to keep your own independent backups. We are not liable for note content lost through synchronisation, deletion, device loss, or interruption of the service.

Where two devices have both changed the same note, the most recently saved version wins and the other is replaced. There is no merge or conflict prompt.

4. Note locking

Note locking restricts access to selected notes within the app, using a PIN or your device's biometric unlock.

It is an access control, not encryption. It does not protect your notes from someone with access to your unlocked device, from your device backups, or from files you export. Exported files are never encrypted.

Your PIN is stored only on your device and cannot be recovered or reset by us. If you forget it, you will lose access to your locked notes. Locked notes are never uploaded to the cloud, so they cannot be recovered from another device either.
